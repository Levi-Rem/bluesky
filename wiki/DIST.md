# DIST: Dist
Distance and direction calculation between two coordinates. The result are the distance in NM and the QDR in degrees.

**Usage:**

    DIST lat0, lon0, lat1, lon1

**Arguments:**

|Name|Type|Required|Description
|--------|------|---|---------------------------------------------------
lat0|float|Yes|[[Latitude|Coordinates]]
lon0|float|Yes|[[Longitude|Coordinates]]
lat1|float|Yes|[[Latitude|Coordinates]]
lon1|float|Yes|[[Longitude|coordinates]]
