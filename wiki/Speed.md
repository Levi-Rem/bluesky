# Entering Speed

Two different formats for entering aircraft velocities are supported:

### Calibrated Airspeed
The standard for interpreting aircraft velocities is the calibrated airspeed, expressed in [knots].

### Mach number
The entered velocity will be interpreted as a Mach number when a number smaller than 1.0 is entered, or when the number is preceded by the letter "M".
