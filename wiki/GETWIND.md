# GETWIND: Getwind
Get wind at a specified position (and optionally at altitude). The result are wind speed [kts] and direction [deg].

**Usage:**

    GETWIND lat,lon,[alt]

**Arguments:**

|Name|Type|Required|Description
|--------|------|---|---------------------------------------------------
lat|float|Yes|[[Latitude|Coordinates]]
lon|float|Yes|[[Longitude|Coordinates]]
alt|float/txt|No|[[Altitude|Altitude]]
