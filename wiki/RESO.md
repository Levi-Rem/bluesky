# RESO: Reso
Set resolution method. There are several available methods that can be used:
- OFF: Do Nothing 
- MVP: [Modified Voltage Potential](http://sesarinnovationdays.eu/files/2016/Papers/SIDs_2016_paper_22.pdf) 
- EBY: [Method developed by Martin S. Eby](https://www.ll.mit.edu/publications/journal/pdf/vol07_no2/7.2.6.selforganizing.pdf) 
- SWARM: [Swarm theory](http://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050912006965)

**Usage:**

    RESO [method]

**Arguments:**

|Name|Type|Required|Description
|--------|------|---|---------------------------------------------------
method|OFF/MVP/EBY/SWARM|Yes|Conflict resolution method
